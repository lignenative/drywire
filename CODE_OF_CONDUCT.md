# Code of conduct

Drywire is built by people who play together, and we want working on it to
feel the same: friendly, direct and fair. This applies everywhere the
project lives: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any chat
we use.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people. Explain your reasoning, and
  assume good faith in others'.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. A review is about the code, not the
  person who wrote it.
- Respect people's time and boundaries. If someone says no, or asks you to
  stop, stop.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language,
  whatever someone's identity, background, experience or level.
- Sexual content or attention, in any form.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, or you see it happen to
someone else, tell the maintainers privately through the contact listed on
the [Ligne Native organization profile](https://github.com/lignenative).
Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled confidentially.

The maintainers may remove comments or contributions, and warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code,
in proportion to what happened. Maintainers who do not follow it in good
faith may be removed from the project.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
